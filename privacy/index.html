Kith

Privacy Policy

Effective 29 October 2026

The short version: Kith collects no data. There is no Kith account, no advertising, no analytics and no tracking. Your accounts, entries, bills and goals stay on your devices and in your own iCloud, where the developer cannot read them.

This policy covers the Kith app for iPhone, iPad, Mac and Apple Watch, including its widgets, made by Dylan Velez (“we”).

Where your data lives

Everything you enter in Kith is stored on your device. Kith syncs it between your devices through your own iCloud account, using Apple’s CloudKit, in your private iCloud database. Only you can open it. We run no servers that hold your data, and we cannot see, read or recover it. Apple’s iCloud terms and privacy policy govern how iCloud stores it.

Sharing a household

If you create a household and invite someone, the accounts, bills and goals you put in the household are stored in a shared iCloud area that you and the people you invited can see. Invitations are iCloud share links that you send yourself, for example in Messages. Your personal money stays in your private database and is never shared. When someone leaves the household, or you end it, their access ends.

On-device intelligence

Features such as talking to Kith, reading a receipt or a statement, and the plain-language monthly summary use Apple’s on-device frameworks: Foundation Models, on-device speech recognition and Vision. That processing happens on your device. Kith never sends your words, images or financial data to a server, to Apple’s Private Cloud Compute or to any third-party model.

Kith asks for the microphone, camera or photo library only when you use a feature that needs it, and you can change those permissions at any time in your device’s Settings.

Exchange rates

To convert between currencies, Kith downloads public exchange rates from the Frankfurter service (api.frankfurter.dev). The request carries only currency codes, with no cookies and no identifiers. It contains no accounts, amounts or anything about you. As with any request on the internet, that service sees the network address it came from.

Diagnostics

Kith’s diagnostics are off unless you turn them on. Even then, they stay on your device, never leave it, and never include your name or any amount. If you choose to contact support, you decide what you send.

Apple may share crash reports with app developers if you allowed that in your device’s Analytics & Improvements settings. Kith adds nothing of yours to them.

Reminders, widgets and Apple Watch

Reminders are scheduled on your device. Widgets and the Apple Watch app show figures from Kith on your own devices. None of this goes through a server of ours.

Deleting your data

You can erase your data from Kith’s Settings. You can also delete the app, and remove Kith’s iCloud data from your device’s iCloud storage settings.

Children

Kith collects no personal information from anyone, children included.

Changes to this policy

If this policy changes, the new version will be posted on this page with a new effective date.
