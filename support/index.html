Support

Something not working, or a question about Kith? Here is how to get help.

Ask a question or report a problem

Fill in the short support form. You will need a free GitHub account, and you will get a reply on the same page.

Open the support form

Requests on the form are public. Please don’t include amounts, account names or other personal details.

Common questions

Do I need an account?

No. Kith has no sign-in. It keeps your data on your device and syncs it through your own iCloud account.

My devices aren’t syncing

Check that every device is signed in to the same Apple Account and that iCloud is turned on for Kith (Settings › your name › iCloud). Sync needs an internet connection and catches up on its own once one is back.

How do I share with my partner?

In Kith, open Settings › Household, create a household, then choose Invite someone. Kith sends an iCloud share link, for example in Messages. Your partner opens it with Kith installed and signed in to their own iCloud.

Why does a feature say it isn’t available on this device?

Talking to Kith and the smart suggestions run on your device with Apple Intelligence. On a device or in a region without it, those features stay hidden or show that message, and everything else works as usual.

Is Kith free?

Yes. Every feature is included, with no in-app purchases, ads or subscriptions.

How do I move to a new device?

Install Kith and sign in to the same Apple Account. Your data arrives through iCloud.

How do I delete my data?

Kith’s Settings can erase it. The Privacy Policy covers what Kith stores and where.
